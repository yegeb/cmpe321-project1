Constraints not expressible in ER:
1. (stadium_name, city) must be UNIQUE
2. (competition name, season) must be UNIQUE
3. attendance ≤ stadium capacity
4. No two matches within 120 min at same stadium
5. No club in two matches within 120 min
6. No referee in two matches within 120 min
7. Max 11 starters per club per match
8. Max 23 squad players per match per club
9. Red/yellow card suspension rules
10. Numeric constraints such as market_value > 0, height_cm > 0, and years_experience ≥ 0 must hold
11. The foundation year of a club must be a valid past year
12. A player may not have two active contracts of the same type at the same time
13. A player may be associated with at most two clubs simultaneously (one permanent and one loan)
14. The end date of a contract must be strictly later than its start date
15. Transfer constraints: Free transfer fee=0; Purchase/Loan fee>0; Loan transfer requires an active Permanent contract with parent club (and parent club must match from_club_id)
16. Manager/Club temporal exclusivity in Leads (a Manager cannot lead two Clubs simultaneously, and a Club cannot have two Managers simultaneously)
17. A person's date_of_birth must be strictly in the past
18. A player cannot appear in the same match as both a home-team and away-team participant
19. 2 yellow cards in a single match automatically result in a red card (derived state)
20. The Match Result (Win/Loss/Draw) is dynamically derived from home/away goals, rather than explicitly stored as an independent attribute
